# Assets

The favicon and application mark share `web/static/favicon.svg`, an original SVG asset.
The interface bundles no subject portraits or historical demonstration images.
Subject imagery, when supplied by research records, must retain source and rights information.

## README screenshots

The three PNG files in `docs/images/` are the user's original Chronicle screenshots,
copied without image edits. They show a researched Céline Dion project; screenshots
illustrate the interface and do not establish historical completeness.

The example project's Céline Dion portrait is
[Celine Dion Live 2017](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Celine_Dion_Live_2017.jpg)
by **Egghead06**, licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The app displays the photograph cropped within its portrait frame.

Video thumbnails in the media screenshot come from videos linked in
[Céline Dion's official video archive](https://www.celinedion.com/video/).
Creator and rights fields remain as recorded in the research project.

Maps include data by [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
The original map attribution remains visible in the screenshots.
